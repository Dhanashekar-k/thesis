### 8.4 Service selection

#### 8.4.1 General

ISO 15118-1 defines use case elements covering different services such as charging, WPT and BPT scenarios. Depending on a use case and a service scenario only one subset of the messages and parameters defined in this document are applied and transmitted by the EVCC and the SECC, respectively.
