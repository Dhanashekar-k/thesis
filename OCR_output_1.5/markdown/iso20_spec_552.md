## Annex I (informative)

## Precision of measurements and tolerances

This document is about the digital communication between the SECC and the EVCC. Some of the communicated values are used as "hard limits" where the violation might result in a termination of the physical energy flow. However, since those values (e.g. power, voltage) are the result of an analog to digital conversion (measurement) which is performed independently on both sides, the software on both sides also needs to be aware of acceptable tolerances.

In many cases normative measurement tolerances related to certain energy transfer techniques may be found in the relevant documents, for example the IEC 61980 or IEC 61851 series. However, in some cases the relevant documents may not yet have defined requirements for the tolerances of all properties.

Table I.1 should only be considered as an informative overview in order to understand the magnitude of acceptable measurement tolerances from the perspective of other energy domains (e.g. metering systems).
