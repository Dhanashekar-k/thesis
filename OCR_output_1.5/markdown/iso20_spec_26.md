While storing any data related to certificates/messages provided by the EVCC, the EVSE and the SAs (including CSO/eMSP/OEM/etc.) should be cognizant of the local/state/national/international privacy rules, regulations and requirements. These rules/regulations/requirements are not in the scope of this document but should be taken into account when designing and implementing the solution.

#### 7.3.2 Certificate and key management

The call flows shown in 7.3.1 require the existence of multiple certificates to be applied for the different security layers. There are SECC certificates used in the TLS layer for the EVCC to authenticate SECC. There are contract certificates used at the application layer to authenticate against an SECC and/or SA. There are V2G root CA certificates and possibly sub-CA certificates which certify SECC certificates and contract certificates.

Separate from above certificates, there are OEM root CA certificates and vehicle certificates used in the TLS layer for the SECC to authenticate the EVCC. OEM provisioning certificates that stem from OEM root CA certificates may be used for installing and updating contract certificates.

Requirements for various certificates can be found in 7.3.2.1. For an overview of all possible certificate profiles, see Annex B. For examples of the certificate structures, see Annex H.

[V2G20-1231] Each V2G entity shall use X.509v3 certificates.
