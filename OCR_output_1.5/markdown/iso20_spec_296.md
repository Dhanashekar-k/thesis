[V2G20-1905] A PriceRule with a PowerRange with positive value shall become active when the current power is at or above the PowerRange value.

[V2G20-1906] A PriceRule with a PowerRange with negative value shall become active when the current power is at or below the PowerRange value.

[V2G20-1908] At least one active PriceRule shall have a PowerRangeStart with a value of zero.

[V2G20-2156] PowerRangeStart shall be greater than or equal to zero if the enclosing AbsolutePriceSchedule is used as a sub-element of a ChargingSchedule.

[V2G20-2157] PowerRangeStart shall be less than or equal to zero if the enclosing AbsolutePriceSchedule is used as a sub-element of a DischargingSchedule.

[V2G20-2158] CarbonDioxideEmission and RenewableGenerationPercentage shall only be used when the enclosing AbsolutePriceSchedule is used as a sub-element of a ChargingSchedule and the PowerRangeStart is equal to zero.
