# Annex D (informative)

## ACDP

### D.1 Coordinate system definition for ACDP with inverted pantograph

For the ACDP with infrastructure mounted pantograph there are two coordinate systems defined:

- the world coordinate system;

– the vehicle coordinate system.

The world coordinate system for the ACDP with infrastructure mounted pantograph defines a right-handed cartesian coordinate system where the x-axis is directed in EVs driving direction.

The definition for the vehicle coordinate system is three-dimensional reference system and fiducial marks, which defines a right handed cartesian coordinate system where the EVs driving direction is counter the x-axis compliant with ISO 4130:1978, Figure D.1.

#### D.1.1 Coordinate system definitions

World coordinate system is referenced to the ACDP, right-hand system in driving direction. Origin of world coordinate system is in the center of the pantograph contacts on street level.

The PPD will provide relative position at  $ d_{x\_PPD}, d_{y\_PPD} $.

Vehicle coordinate system as defined in ISO 4130:

 $ X = \text{positive in the rearward direction} $;

Y = positive to the right side of the vehicle;

Z = positive in the upwards direction;

X = 0 and Y = 0 are defined by the center of the contact rails (ACDP counterpart) on the vehicle respectively;

 $ Z = 0 $ is the height of the contact rails to the ground at street level.

NOTE 1 While vehicle approaching the PPD x-coordinates are negative. When passing the center of the ACDP the x-coordinates become positive.

NOTE 2 The ACDP contact matching condition is:

absolute value of EVRelative_X_Deviation is less than ContactWindowXc;

absolute value of EVRelativeYDeviation is less than ContactWindowYc.
