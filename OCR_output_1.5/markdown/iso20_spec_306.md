[V2G20-1820] In the context of ThreePhase energy transfer, if an EV is only capable of symmetric energy transfer, it shall during the ChargeParameterDiscoveryReq only communicate an EVMaximumChargePower and EVMimimumChargePower, which represent the sum of all three phases in accordance to requirement [V2G20-1814].

[V2G20-1821] In the context of ThreePhase energy transfer, if an EV is capable of asymmetric energy transfer, it shall during the ChargeParameterDiscoveryReq communicate an EVMaximumChargePower and EVMinimumChargePower with phase specific power values (see 8.3.5.2.2).

NOTE If the EVCC only sent the base elements in case of ThreePhase energy transfer in the ChargeParameterDiscoveryReq, it implicitly states that it is only capable of symmetric energy transfer. In case it sends the phase specific elements with the suffixes L2 and L3, it implicitly states, that it is capable of asymmetric energy transfer.

[V2G20-1209] SECC shall not send _L2, _L3 values in any messages (ChargeParameterDiscoveryRes, AC_ChargeLoopRes) if it neither received _L2 nor _L3 values in ChargeParameterDiscoveryReq.

[V2G20-1822] In the context of ThreePhase energy transfer, if an EV is only capable of 2 phase energy transfer, it shall during the ChargeParameterDiscoveryReq communicate its capabilities in accordance to requirement [V2G20-1821] and mark the unused phase by using the value zero.
